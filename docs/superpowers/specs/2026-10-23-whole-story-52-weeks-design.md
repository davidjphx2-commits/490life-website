# The Whole Story: 52 Weekly Lessons

Approved by David, 2026-10-23 (brainstorm: approach B, map approved, weekly pattern approved, rollout with "replace the 26 entirely").

## Why
Each of the 26 lessons (2,300 to 5,500 words; 9 to 16 PDF pages) is too much for a new reader to take in at once. The study becomes a year-long, 52-week course of smaller, complete weekly lessons.

## Source of truth
The 26 lessons in `Bible Study Project/lessons/NN-slug.body.html`, their diagrams, images, guides (`guides/NN.json`) and Core Doctrine boxes (`tools/doctrines.json`). Every week is built from its lesson's existing text, rearranged, plus a small amount of new writing. No week adds new teaching content beyond openings, Week A Turns, recaps, and split practices and questions.

## A week
- Cover: "Week N of 52", title, passage, one engraving from the lesson (A and B use different ones).
- You are here strip (the lesson's).
- Opening: Week A new (about 100 words); Week B a "Last week…" recap plus the new question (about 100 words).
- The Word card: Week A.
- The Parts in the week's range, with their diagrams and images.
- Core Doctrine box: stays where it already sits in the story (its anchor in `doctrines.json`), so it may fall in A or B.
- Thread Watch: Week B.
- The Turn: Week A gets a new short Turn (about 150 words) that ends at Christ; Week B keeps the lesson's Turn.
- Living It: 3 practices each (the lesson's six split, rewritten where needed); the memory verse goes in Week B.
- For Discussion: 3 or 4 questions each.
- Before next week: the reading for the next week.
- Sources: Week B only.
- Small Group Guide: 2 pages. Page 1 for the group (big idea, reading, open question, 3 fill-ins, 3–4 discussion questions, memory verse, pray). Page 2 leader notes (45-minute plan, 3 main points, answer key, core doctrine if the week has one, one hard question with answer, closing prayer).

## Site, PDFs, Drive
- The 52 weeks become The Whole Story at /bible-study/, listed by unit. Week URLs: /bible-study/week-NN-slug/.
- The 26 full lessons come off the site once all 52 are live (David's choice). Each old lesson URL becomes a forwarding page to its Week A so shared links keep working. The 26 lesson PDFs on the Drive: delete or keep is asked at that time (irreversible).
- Each week: lesson PDF `490-life-study-guide-week-NN-slug.pdf` and guide `490-life-small-group-guide-week-NN-slug.pdf`, on the site and the Drive ("490 Life - The Whole Story - Week NN - Title …").
- Showcase PDF (Michael) and "At a Glance" summary updated to 52 weeks after Unit 6 ships.

## Rollout and checks
Unit by unit: build, David reviews, publish on his approval. Each unit: ESV verification of every quote, zero em-dashes, theology review of all new writing, page-by-page visual QA of every PDF, site build plus smoke and link tests, live verification, Drive size check, vault and memory updates.

## Voice and theology
David's voice (no em-dashes, no AI tics). All existing guardrails hold: Westminster frame, common ground on baptism and the Lord's Supper with "Where churches differ", definite atonement never contradicted while Christ is freely offered, Romans 5:12 read as sinning in Adam, eternal punishment stated, God the Father never pictured.
